ToDi: Token-wise Distillation via
Fine-Grained Divergence Control

Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) offer impressive performance but are impractical for resource-constrained deployment due to high latency and energy consumption. Knowledge distillation (KD) offers a promising solution, but existing methods apply a fixed divergence objective across all tokens, failing to account for token-level variation in the teacher-student relationship. We propose ToDi, which dynamically combines Forward KL and Reverse KL divergences at the token level using sigmoid-based weighting derived from teacher-student probability ratios. This enables precise distribution alignment and consistently outperforms existing distillation approaches on instruction-following benchmarks.
