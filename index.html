Seongryong Jung

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Chung-Ang University. My research interests include post-training, knowledge distillation, and agentic AI for large language models.

I am especially interested in making capable language models more efficient and practical through fine-grained learning signals and reliable training recipes.

Research

My current work focuses on efficient LLM training and distillation, with an emphasis on token-level objectives, post-training, and evaluation of instruction-following models.

ToDi
 ToDi: Token-wise Distillation via Fine-Grained Divergence Control
Seongryong Jung, Suwan Yoon, DongGeon Kim, Hwanhee Lee
EMNLP, 2025
paper / arXiv / code

ToDi adaptively combines forward and reverse KL divergence per token, improving knowledge distillation for instruction-following language models.
