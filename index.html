Seongryong Jung

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Chung-Ang University, Seoul, Korea.

I have had research experience at Trillion Labs and DMT Labs, where both experiences centered on training models with awareness of their current state of knowledge, through continual learning across multiple domains at Trillion Labs and knowledge distillation at DMT Labs.

My long-term goal is to build self-evolving AI systems that can autonomously and continuously improve themselves. I am particularly drawn to post-training methods that adapt to a model's current state, as a stepping stone toward AI that can guide and improve its own learning.
