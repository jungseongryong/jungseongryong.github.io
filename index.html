Seongryong Jung

I am a research intern at Princeton University under the supervision of Prof. Zhuang Liu. I am also an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at Chung-Ang University in Seoul, Korea.

My research focuses on scaling agentic intelligence to increasingly complex and open-ended tasks, with the broader goal of building self-improving AI systems that continually learn from their own experiences. I am also interested in open foundation models and how they can make advanced AI more accessible and extensible.

At Trillion Labs, I worked on foundation model post-training, with a focus on knowledge distillation and continual learning for large language models. My prior work was nominated for an outstanding paper award at EMNLP.
